AUTUMN RECIPES

Pumpkin Soup

Ingredients:
Serves: 2
Pumpkin 500g
Onion 1 piece
Water 500ml
Salt and Pepper
Steps:
Cut the pumpkin and onion into small pieces.
Boil them in water until soft.
Blend everything together and add salt and pepper.

Baked Apples

Ingredients:
Serves: 2
