Pumpkin Soup
Ingredients:
Serves: 2
Pumpkin 500g
Onion 1 peace
Water 500ml
Salt and Pepper
Method to the recipe:
Cut the pumpkin and onion into small pieces.
Boil them in water until soft.
Blend everything together and add salt and pepper.

Baked Apples
Ingredients:
Serves: 2
2 apples
1 teaspoon cinnamon
1 teaspoon honey
Method to recipe:
Cut the apples and remove the cores.
Add cinnamon and honey. Bake at 180C for 20 min.
