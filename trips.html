OUR PICKS

Prague

Prague is beautiful in autumn, especially in its park
and historic center. Places like Petřín, Stromovka
and the area around the Vltava River are perfect
for an autumn walk.

Šumava

Šumava is full of colorful forest, hills and beautiful views in autumn.
its great places for hiking, taking photos and enjoying nature.

Krkonoše

The krkonoše Mountains offer beautiful
autumn scenery and many hiking trails.
From higher places, you can enjoy amazing views of
the surrounding countryside

Český Krumlov

Český Krumlov has beautiful historic center
and a peaceful autumn atmosphere
The colorful trees around the town make
it a great place for day trip
