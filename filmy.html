Hocus Pocus

Hocus Pocus is funny fantasy movie that takes place
around Halloween. Three witches are accidentally
brought back to life after many years.
Three children try to stop them before they can
cause trouble in their town. The movie has a
magical, funny and spooky atmosphere,
it is perfect for cozy autumn evening.

Fantastic Mr. Fox

Fantastic Mr. Fox is an animated movie
about a clever fox who wants to provide food for
his family. He starts stealing from three angry farmers,
but they decided to catch him.
the movie is funny and entertaining,
with beautiful autumn colors and cozy atmosphere.
It's a great choice for watching with family or friends.
